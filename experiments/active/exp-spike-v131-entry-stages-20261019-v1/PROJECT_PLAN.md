# Entry-stage comparison

Owner authorized all four entry stages on 2026-10-19. One factor varies: the
closed-bar stage permitting the next-open entry. Immediate original signal,
first breakout close, first valid retest close, and existing rebreak close are
fixed before the run. The latter three retain the original 24-bar deadline and
cancel priority; breakout and retest must occur on different bars. Do not wait
to observe whether an earlier entry later completes confirmation.

- Reuse all 638 registered Binance symbols and four complete chart grids. Read
  migrated original paths; record sizes/mtimes and real reads, no content hashes.
- Rebuild existing signal facts and verify original candidate identities. Keep
  original five-bar/0.2ATR/2ATR absolute stop, actual-entry R, close-2R arming,
  next-bar 2ATR trail on 1H and 4ATR on other grids, raw opposite next-open exit,
  and fixed 20bp cost. No new stop, sizing, fee, detector or live setting.
- Each policy independently replays both sides with one position per symbol and
  timeframe, warming from November 2022 before reporting entries from January
  2023. Earlier exits can admit new signals; occupied confirmations expire.
  This is equal-risk trade accounting, not shared-capital account simulation.
- Reuse the deterministic original random anchor draw by trade ID, same symbol,
  side, UTC week, temporal fold and causal ATR/price bin. Verify every available
  archived draw. Extend that identical method to previously unrecorded anchors;
  never redraw a failed/canceled/Sunday control. Apply the same non-Sunday anchor
  guard to controls. Known no-fill outcomes remain zero; missing/censored is not
  zero. All four policies share one draw per original signal.
- Report independent matched opportunities separately from paired serial
  accounting. For paired serial, first restrict both anchor queues to ex-ante
  available non-Sunday matches, then run occupancy independently on each queue.
  A skipped filled event realizes zero at its decision, not its hypothetical exit.
  Do not compare full signal sums with an unmatched random subset.
- Early results require entry/anchor and all required outcomes before 2025;
  later starts at/after the split, crossing observations explicitly excluded.
  Common matched samples require all four policy outcomes known, for fair
  ranking; serial-skipped hypothetical censored trades are known zero. History
  has already been examined, so this is exploratory temporal robustness.
- Select a uniform policy by early total serial net R and evaluate the later
  period; also show predefined timeframe-specific selection. No outcome-based
  symbol exclusions, new thresholds or combination with staged-order search.
- Validate decision prefix invariance and mirror symmetry, cancellation priority,
  occupancy release/replacement and next-open execution; reconcile original
  rebreak candidates and 15m/30m/4H serial ledgers. 1H actual 2ATR serial is a new
  baseline and must be separated from old fixed-entry 1H statistics.
