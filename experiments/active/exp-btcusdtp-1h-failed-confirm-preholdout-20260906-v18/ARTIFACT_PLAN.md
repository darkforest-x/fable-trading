## Report spine and required-structure mapping

1. Title: plain-English artifact name; matching first visible heading.
2. Technical summary: actual result and implication, written after validation.
3. Scope and definitions before findings: original cases/controls, fixed pair
   denominator, costs, units, time folds, V17 versus new candidate. Reordered
   ahead of findings so aggregate returns cannot be mistaken for account PnL.
4. Key findings: net/PF/halves and own matched controls with exact audit table;
   adjacent narrative interprets absolute versus relative improvement.
5. Dedicated specification: K1 entry gate, native5 pending and native15 exits,
   next-open/hard-stop clocks; original rule versus the only changed branch.
6. Distribution chart then decomposition: improvements, deeper losses,
   recovered winners, cancellations, partial restoration, residuals/unknowns.
7. Methods and validation: source/context-before-outcome freeze, ledger
   arithmetic, shared denominator, serial recomputation and chronological
   inference. Exact commands remain because owner explicitly requires them.
8. Limitations/robustness: unchanged gates, cost30bp, tails, normality/time
   dependence, repeated research, missing matched support and scope of QA.
9. Next steps and further questions: combined because both depend on whether
   this mechanism survives full-population and controls; no fabricated result.
