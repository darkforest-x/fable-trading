# SPIKE V7/V8 noise-reduction execution — delivery contract

This umbrella experiment records the owner's request to execute five previously
proposed lines of work.  They remain separate causal questions rather than one
bundled entry filter:

1. one preregistered market-breadth hard gate plus matched controls;
2. cross-venue event folding and portfolio-cap accounting;
3. four separately reported early-failure exit candidates;
4. one fixed 30-day, 0.80 return-correlation portfolio gate; and
5. a forward-only V7/V8 shadow ledger.

Historical inputs cover the already researched two-year V7/V8 replay.  Any
configuration that reads observations on or after 2026-05-04 records its own
authorized holdout use #1; those results are nonblind reused-history evidence.
The market-breadth hard gate uses the development evidence declared by its
source experiment.  The forward observer consumes no historical outcome and
starts from an immutable activation time.

Acceptance here means that each requested question has a reproducible result,
an explicit null/control, a receipt chain, a risk statement and a disposition.
It does not accept a trading rule.  No result may change Bark, Telegram, YOLO,
Pine, an active model, an order, a position or a production threshold.  The
forward shadow remains observation-only until the owner separately authorizes
a production decision after enough new outcomes mature.
