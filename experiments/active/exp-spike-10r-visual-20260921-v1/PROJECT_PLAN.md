# 10R winners: retrospective visual morphology audit

## Question and frozen scope

Inspect all 448 original V9 long candidates with final net R strictly above 10.
These are candidate outcomes, not 448 independently executable portfolio trades.
Select one non-winner per winner where possible, matching exact venue/symbol/timeframe,
UTC quarter and fixed ATR-fraction bucket (.005/.01/.02/.05/.1 boundaries), excluding
the nearest max(24 native bars,24 hours). Draw controls without replacement with seed
921601; report unmatched cases without widening the match. Include 48 concealed repeats.
The sole new measurement is visual morphology; do not change trading rules or costs.

## Causal rendering and blinding

Physically truncate OHLC and original six close-MA columns at signal close. The 128-bar
overview and 32-bar detail share no future bars. No entry/exit/profit/asset/date is shown.
Opaque randomized IDs prevent outcome-driven visual grading. Keep identity and outcomes
in private files. Reviewers read only blind images and this rubric until labels freeze.
Missing/warmup/gap charts remain unknown rather than being discarded.

## Manual visual rubric, frozen before rendering or outcomes

Compare the endpoint against the owner ETH arrow reference, recovered owner positives
and difficult negatives. The direction-aligned LONG interpretation is research judgment,
not newly owner-confirmed Gold. Do not enforce the old automated post5 release filter:
bars after the signal are unavailable. Judge only a core near the right edge, within the
last approximately 8 bars; a dense patch far to the left does not qualify.

- **P (strict candidate)**: all three SMA/EMA families visibly meet in one compact bundle,
  with a preceding convergence/settling phase; nearby candles touch or hug that bundle;
  a fresh upward departure starts near the endpoint, without an already extended run,
  violent sawtooth action or a visibly isolated slow pair. This is a candidate resembling
  the desired perfect launch, not a trade recommendation or confirmed Gold label.
- **D (generic density)**: all six visibly gather near the endpoint/recent core, but
  fresh/clean launch, quiet setup or proximity is inadequate. State the failing axis.
- **B (partial/borderline)**: apparent compression mainly involves fast/mid lines, a
  slow line remains apart, or convergence is suggestive but not a clear six-line bundle.
- **N (no target)**: separated lines, an established trend/pullback or rebound without
  a recent six-line convergence. A profitable continuation is not a dense launch.
- **U (unknown)**: missing data or rendering prevents a defensible visual judgment.

Record one class and a short visible reason per exposure, before reading private outcome
files. Read sheets at original resolution; open individual cards when necessary.
No feature threshold may substitute for actual image inspection. Freeze primary labels
before unblinding; disagreements and concealed repeat consistency are results, not labels
to be silently corrected after seeing profits. A second reader may review a disjoint blind
subset and an overlapping subset; disclose reader identity and stratify sensitivity.

## Analysis and decision

Report strict P and broad P+D rates separately in all winners and matched non-winners,
by timeframe and reader. Use paired label-swapping or exact discordant-pair tests for
the matched comparison; repeated/nearby cross-venue events are correlated, so also report
asset episode groups (adjacent selected events <=24h) as a descriptive sensitivity.
Case-control sampling cannot estimate P(>10R | shape) directly. No AUC, top-decile return,
portfolio backtest or economic win rate is generated by this non-directional label audit;
these metrics are not applicable. Its null is equal morphology prevalence across matched
outcome groups, plus hidden-repeat consistency. Existing outcome barriers/costs stay frozen.

The audit may justify collecting causal, owner-confirmed LONG Gold and difficult negatives.
It cannot itself authorize YOLO training, supply bounding boxes, or establish profitability.
training_eligible and production_eligible stay false. Prior high mAP from future movement
is an explicit failure mode, not evidence for training again on the same targets.
