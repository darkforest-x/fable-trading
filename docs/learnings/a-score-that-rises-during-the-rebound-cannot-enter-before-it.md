# A score that rises during the rebound cannot enter before it

- **Problem**: The Monday 1.618 touch-bar score works only with the touch 5m bar's close and full volume. The owner asked whether following the score trade by trade through that bar, and entering at market as soon as it first clears the threshold, keeps the backtest's effect.
- **Dead end**: Scoring once at the first touching trade (10-29) selected fewer, weaker trades. Following the score through the bar looked like a fix, because it eventually matches the stored score (last-trade difference ≤ 0.002). It does not help. Of the 498 crossings of 0.5, 331 happen at the first trade, so they are the same touches as the instant test. The 167 later crossings fill far from the level: median +139bp at 0.5 and +290bp at 0.6, after 100 ms. Or they come after price already reached the target: 53 of 94 later crossings of 0.6 occurred after the Monday edge was hit. Over the last five months, v3 ends at 1.25x (1.33x at zero latency) against 1.70x resting on the pre-touch score. Spike ends at 0.91x against 1.52x.
- **Effective path**: Split the crossings by decision offset and check the fill shift and the target-first flags. This shows the score rises with the volume and close of the rebound itself. It describes the rebound instead of predicting it. Waiting for it moves the entry either into the extended part of the move or past the move entirely.
- **General rule**: Before trying to reach a fill-bar score sooner, ask which part of the bar raises it. If it rises with the outcome (rebound volume, close back inside), no earlier clock can recover its effect. Measure where the entry lands at the first crossing, not only how many events cross.
