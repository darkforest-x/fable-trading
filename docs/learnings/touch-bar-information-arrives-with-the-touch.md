# Deciding inside the touch bar cannot recover the touch-bar leak: the useful information arrives with the touch

- **Problem**: The stored Monday1618 entry score reads the full touch bar ([ledger-entry-i-is-the-fill-bar-not-the-submission-bar.md](ledger-entry-i-is-the-fill-bar-not-the-submission-bar.md)). On the previous close the score drops 243 of the 2108 v3 parents, and those 243 are the best ones (+361bp vs +222bp kept). The owner asked whether scoring at minute 3 or 4 of the 5m bar, then resting the limit, keeps them.
- **Dead ends**:
  - Assuming the touch usually comes late in the bar. On Binance UM 1m, the touch minute within the touch 5m bar is spread: 21% minute 0, 28% minute 1, 21% minute 2, 16% minute 3, 15% minute 4.
  - Hoping the partial score is the missing piece. At minute 3 the partial score clears 0.5 for 93% of the in-time events. But of the 243, 194 had already touched before minute 3, and 222 before minute 4. Deciding at minute 3 recovers 14 of the 243, and at minute 4 only 8.
- **What worked**: Splitting every lost event into "touched before the decision" vs "score below at the decision". This shows the leak's value comes from the spike that is the touch itself, not from late-bar drift. A 1m rebuild of the full bar reproduced every stored score exactly (1m high/low and volume matched the 5m bars in all 2107 events). The split therefore measures timing only, not data differences.
- **General rule**: When a feature leaks the fill bar, check when within that bar the fill happens before trying to "decide a bit later". If the information is generated by the move that fills the order, no in-bar decision time recovers it. The only causal use is after the fill, for an exit decision.
