# A raw detector replay cannot replace the existing pipeline baseline

- **问题**：Owner asked why the morphology v6 Arm A replay produced so many apparent signals and why prior YOLO work was not reused. The 2026-09-22 top-gainer replay used the v6 A checkpoint, while the notification monitor still uses the Grade-A full40 checkpoint. These are separate model paths.
- **死胡同**：Describe v6 A as a new mainline version, count repeated raw boxes as accepted signals, or explain every questionable box as a display duplicate. The replay intentionally omitted semantic and economic acceptance gates; its 773 raw boxes across 729 of 5,250 scored windows became 92 overlapping review regions, not validated trade events. Clear-background training and sparse high-timeframe coverage are further limitations, not a measured explanation of every false detection.
- **有效路径**：Trace the v6 lineage through the 3R-positive experiment and the correction of invalid outcome-negative labels. Its actual Arm A `args.yaml` starts from `models/yolo11s.pt`, not the prior trained Grade-A checkpoint. A uses one view per event; B uses two. Old reviewed rows protect negative-sampling regions but are not thereby reused as supervision. Compare the raw replay's explicit no-semantic-gate contract with the earlier paired semantic-gate evaluation, and check the live runtime SHA separately. The old evaluation established limited 15m false-positive reduction, not blanket deployment eligibility.
- **通用规则**：A new research arm must state exactly which prior weights, labels, hard negatives, feature contracts, and postprocessing it inherits or omits. Preserve an unchanged full-pipeline baseline on the same frozen market inputs before interpreting raw-model observations as an improvement. Diagnostic ablations may omit gates, but must not silently redefine the user's signal workflow. Reuse an old classifier only after checking its target and candidate distribution.
