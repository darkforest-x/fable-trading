# Don't exclude a subgroup from an entry rule using outcomes measured at a different entry price

- **Problem**: Before testing market entries at the Monday1618 touch close, I proposed entering only when the touch bar had closed back inside the level. My evidence was a descriptive count. The 642 high-score touches that closed at or beyond the level averaged −1.0% (win 23%), but that was measured on the old limit fills at the level price with v3 exits. The owner asked why we needed the restriction.
- **Dead ends**: Treating the count as a verdict on the new rule. A market entry at that close buys about 0.9% better than the level for a long that has not reclaimed (median). The reclaim and breakeven are still judged against the level, so a later reclaim lifts the stop to a price above the entry. Measured in the framework (`exp-monday-limit-close-reclaim-20261028-v1`) these entries were positive: near-edge target +102bp (win 62%), opposite 1618 +73bp. Adding them doubled the v3-like compounding from 1.78x to 3.39x since 2020.
- **What worked**: Ran the excluded group as its own variant in the same framework run instead of dropping it, then compared on identical events, fees and slippage.
- **General rule**: An outcome statistic is a property of the entry price, exits and rule it was measured under. Before using it to filter a new rule, rerun the subgroup under the new rule, or at least ask whether the new entry price changes its sign. If the owner questions a filter, make it a variant rather than defending it.
