# Paper account variants need their own protection configuration

- **问题**：v3lock shares v3 entry, score tiers, targets and sizing, but owns a different profit-lock offset. The snapshot keeps the original 17 bp offset at the top level and the new 50 bp offset under `v3lock`; blindly sharing all v3 display logic would misstate the new account's rule.
- **死胡同**：Adding an option to the homepage selector alone would leave cold-link symbol selection and analytics identity validation on their original allowlists. Reusing the global offset would display 0.17%, and deriving protection from the current configuration would overwrite the meaning of older stored positions. These shortcuts were rejected during contract inspection.
- **有效路径**：Map the account key to its internal identity (4060) across bootstrap, ledger, analytics and close-dialog labels. Share only the unchanged v3 settings; select the protection offset from the account's own configuration. Render each position's saved `be_price` and activation time, including short positions. Missing accounts remain unavailable instead of borrowing v3 or 60m balances.
- **通用规则**：Before integrating a strategy variant, list which settings are inherited and which are overridden, then trace the account identity through every read path. Verify both a competing original account and missing-account responses. Stitch supplies presentation, while the backend contract owns confirmation periods, direction and account semantics.
- **牵连**：`monday-reclaim-paper.js`, `startup-hints.js`, `reclaim-analytics.js`, `reclaim-close.js`; v3lock 4060, 50 bp versus original 17 bp. Targeted tests and read-only browser fixtures verify isolation and stored long/short protection prices without changing trading behavior.
