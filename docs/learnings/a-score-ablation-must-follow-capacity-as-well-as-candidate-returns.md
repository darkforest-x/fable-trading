# A score ablation must follow capacity as well as candidate returns

- **Problem**: The owner asked whether pre-touch 1m scoring or removing the 0.6 score gate could recover the old spike result. The saved 1m model improved ranking, while its funded account deteriorated; no-score candidates could also have positive arithmetic returns alongside a losing account.
- **Dead end**: Treating AUC, candidate win rate, or average independent return as the account outcome. Comparing different BE offsets or fixed-seed versus median allocations also obscures the requested baseline. Dropping missing-score events silently would leave a residual score-availability filter in the supposedly unfiltered arm.
- **Effective path**: Freeze EMA >= 3, near Monday edge, P18/BE0/H1, maker/taker fees, non-target slippage and the original fixed-seed ten-slot/five-times allocator. Reuse 2002 exact native rows and execute only 6264 missing positions across OHLC/OLHC. Reproduce the original 138/166 wins and 5032.0987915 USDT ledger exactly. In 2026 restarts, causal 5m >= 0.6 ends 1091.64 USDT; saved causal 1m >= 0.6 ends 601.85; no-score ends 611.52. The no-score arm has 447 candidates averaging +57.36bp, but only 378 obtain slots; the 69 skipped average +385.54bp, the funded native cohort averages -2.54bp, and the original liquidation proxy lowers its average to -9.81bp. This is descriptive allocation attribution, not a counterfactual claim that every missed winner was executable. The common score-known no-score universe still loses in 2026 (846.42 USDT), so missing scores alone do not explain the conclusion.
- **General rule**: Report admission, independent outcomes, funded outcomes and rejected-by-capacity outcomes separately. A higher-frequency selector can remove the account's access to concentrated profitable events. Keep the original baseline and controls, expose missing-score coverage, and do not equate the numeric 0.6 cut across separately fitted models. The observed failure of this model/cut/allocation combination does not prove that 1m information is useless or that 0.6 is optimal.
- **Dependencies and limits**: `yoyo/evaluation/spike_score_ablation.py`, `exp-spike-score-ablation-20261030-v1`, original `slot_replay`, frozen 1m model trained before 2026. No retraining, threshold search or production change. Worst-path independent fills and the realized-equity/MAE overlay retain historical execution/margin limits; no funding, queue or latency simulation. 2026 matched no-score excess is +54.86bp, week-sign p=0.1665 and 95% interval [-32.93,127.08]bp; it does not establish a reliable excess return.
