# When a pre-registered selection finds nothing, that is the result: don't relax the rule to rescue a candidate

- **Problem**: The 1m entry score ranked better on unseen data (2025-to-date AUC 0.688 vs 0.648, top decile +42 vs +4bp). The owner asked for a strategy designed around it. The design grid had 162 combos (cut, 15m EMA order filter, target, stop). The selection rule was written into the config before any strategy result was seen: on 2025, with a model that never saw 2025, take the best multiple among combos with at least 40 trades, drawdown at most 50%, and a positive net in both halves. Then test once on 2026.
- **Dead ends**:
  - Treating the label-level gain as a guarantee. Under 10 slots at 3x, all 162 combos lost in 2025: best 0.61x, median 0.26x, every drawdown above 50%, and 12 to 25 liquidations each.
  - Some combos are positive per trade (+73bp over 1631 trades), which tempts a switch to lower leverage, or the "best of the losers". Both are post-hoc rule changes. They would have to be tested on 2026, the only remaining period, which has already been looked at many times.
- **What worked**: The protocol said that if nothing qualifies, nothing is tested. The summary was extended only to record why each combo failed. Drawdown failed for all 162; the first half failed for 146. The rule itself was not changed, and the first summary is kept.
- **General rule**: Write the design space, sizing and selection rule into a committed config before any result exists. If no candidate passes, report the failure and stop that line, or find a genuinely new unseen period (forward paper). Never re-select on the test period. A label metric (AUC, top decile on one exit) is necessary but far from sufficient for a leveraged, compounding strategy.
