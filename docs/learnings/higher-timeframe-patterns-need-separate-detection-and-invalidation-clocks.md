# Higher-timeframe patterns need separate detection and invalidation clocks

- **Problem**: Monday1618 still touches on the original5m clock, while a15m or1h harmonic detector can only confirm points after complete higher bars. The last observable higher close may precede the actual decision boundary by5–55minutes.
- **Rejected shortcut**: Merely changing the interval name or slicing a fully aggregated history at the touch timestamp would admit the unfinished higher candle, whose high/low/close can contain future prices. Stopping every check at the previous higher close would instead ignore a zone failure already observable in the intervening5m bars. Neither interpretation preserves the original causal candidate semantics.
- **Effective path**: Keep two clocks. Pattern construction ends at `floor(touch5m_open, higher_interval)` and requires every underlying5m constituent; no interpolation or partial aggregate is accepted. Only completed5m bars between that higher close and the original touch boundary extend the invalidation checks. They cannot form or confirm higher pivots. XABC confirmation and all pattern point closes are audited against the original boundary.
- **General rule**: Increasing a feature's bar interval changes when its structural evidence becomes available. It does not erase more recent facts that can invalidate it. Freeze construction and invalidation clocks separately, and mutate the unfinished aggregate's future constituents to test the boundary.
- **Evidence and scope**: `tests/evaluation/test_monday_harmonic.py` covers15m/1h positive candidates, future mutation, prefix truncation, incomplete constituents and pre-touch tail expiry. Eighteen real5m windows retain identical raw candidates and gates. The two separately registered experiments keep1000bars/10spacing/3percent ratios and all original trading rules; this causal correctness does not establish profitability.
