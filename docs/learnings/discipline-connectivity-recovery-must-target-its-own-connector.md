# Recover discipline connectivity through its own connector

- **Problem**: On 2026-10-20 at approximately 19:55 Beijing time, the discipline App could not connect. Local `127.0.0.1:8897/health` returned 200, but `https://discipline.spikeok.com/health` and `/v1/state` returned HTTP 530 with Cloudflare error 1033. The separate main website remained reachable.
- **Dead end**: The discipline connector process was running and its local `/ready` returned 200 with two ready connections. Provider inspection nevertheless reported no active connection for that tunnel. Local readiness alone could not establish phone connectivity; restarting the trading backend would not address the observed failing layer.
- **Effective path**: Reconnect only `com.spike.discipline.cloudflared` with `launchctl kickstart -k`. Provider inspection then showed a newly registered connector. Public health, authenticated state and account reads returned 200; unauthenticated state returned 401 and the unexposed root returned 404. The state had a 3.7-second heartbeat age, a 2.4-second account update age, successful account synchronization, and 499 instruments and quotes. The trading backend and main website service PIDs remained unchanged. No trading command, credential change or backend restart was performed.
- **General rule**: Resolve the failing hostname to its exact connector before recovery. Check local origin, provider registration and actual authenticated public reads separately. Preserve the independent authentication boundary and trading service lifecycle. This recovery establishes server-side connectivity, not a verified phone-network result or a permanent cure; the cause of the stale provider registration remains unknown.
- **Related**: `yoyo/discipline_app/scripts/public_service.py`, `yoyo/discipline_app/api.py`, and [the earlier website registration divergence](tunnel-local-readiness-can-diverge-from-provider-registration.md). Recovery command: `launchctl kickstart -k "gui/$(id -u)/com.spike.discipline.cloudflared"`.
