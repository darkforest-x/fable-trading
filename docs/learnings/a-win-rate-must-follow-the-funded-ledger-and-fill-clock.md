- **Problem**: The owner asked where the displayed spike 83.1 percent win rate came from and how its entries were distributed. The same strategy also had a published 85.39 percent candidate win rate, making cohort confusion easy.
- **Dead end**: Quoting the 219 independent candidates, an annual restart, or a median over tie seeds as if it described the displayed continuous account. Grouping only by equal entry/exit timestamps also mixes same-bar target wins with same-bar stop losses.
- **Effective path**: Read the immutable baseline ledger from `exp-spike-booklet-close-compare-20261029-v1`, select `notional > 0`, and join its unique trade IDs to `exp-monday-limit-spike-hw-20261026-v1/trades.csv`. Its 53 skipped candidates comprise 49 wins and four losses, all on three Beijing dates: 2023-11-10 (6), 2024-02-29 (20), and 2026-08-22 (27). For the funded ledger, require both equal entry/exit candle timestamps and target exit reason 4: 96 same-bar target wins booked +4665.411538 USDT; the remaining 70 trades booked -633.312747 USDT. Their sum equals the original +4032.098791 USDT. This is descriptive accounting under the original allocation, not a counterfactual strategy-removal result.
- **General rule**: Resolve displayed performance to exact funded trade IDs before explaining distributions. Preserve allocation, same-time tie seed/order, timezone, fees, and win definition. Distinguish same-bar target exits from all same-bar exits, and never imply that the recorded bar timestamp proves a seconds-level fill. A score known only at the touch close cannot authorize an earlier touch fill.
