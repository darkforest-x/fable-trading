# Refitting the entry score without the leaky bar improves its ranking but not the strategy it drives

- **Problem**: The frozen Monday1618 score had been fitted on features that read the touch bar. The owner asked for a refit on the bar before the touch, trained before 2026 and tested on 2026 to date, followed by a rerun of the strategies.
- **Dead ends**:
  - Taking the label-level gain as the answer. On 2026 v2 near-edge fills the refit scores AUC 0.661 (permutation p 0.001) and top decile +21bp, against +17bp for the frozen model on the same causal features. In the framework, however, v3 A in 2026 (out of sample for both models) gives 1.00x and +48bp per fill for the refit, against 1.14x and +87bp for the frozen causal score. spike falls from 1.19x to 0.81x and v3add20 from 1.41x to 1.13x. The label is the v2 near-edge exit. The strategies use different exits, targets, thresholds and sizing, so a better rank on one exit does not carry over.
  - Keeping 0.5 / 0.6 as probability cuts and expecting the same trades. The refit's probabilities are compressed, so ≥ 0.5 selects 1612 events instead of 2292. Its coefficient on vol_ratio fell from 0.23 to 0.03, because without the touch bar the volume burst is gone. With the touch bar it scores lower, since toward24 is negative. Layer B (exit when the touch-close score < 0.5) therefore closes 454 fills and turns ABC from an improvement into a loss (2.33x vs 3.50x for A).
  - Reading 2025 for the refit. 2025 is inside its training window: 0.81x there against 0.61x for the frozen model says nothing.
- **What worked**: A reproduction control first. The same procedure with the touch-bar values must give the frozen coefficients, and did so to 1e-16. Then compare both models only on the period neither saw (2026) and at the strategy level, through the same framework fills and sizing. Shared trade ids must match the earlier runs (2524 and 7017 rows, 0 mismatches).
- **General rule**: Judge a model change by the strategy result in a period neither model trained on, not by AUC or top-decile on the training label. Re-examine every threshold and every layer that reads the score, because the score's scale and its behaviour on later bars change with the refit.
