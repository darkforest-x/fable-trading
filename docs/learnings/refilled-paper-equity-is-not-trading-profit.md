# Refilled paper equity is not trading profit

- **问题**：The allin3 paper account can restore equity to its initial 1000 USDT after a loss and increment its stake count. The old UI computed realized P&L as equity minus initial equity, so a restored balance would erase the displayed trading loss.
- **死胡同**：Subtracting `injected` is also wrong for transaction P&L: that field is the stake-budget denominator (1000 × stakes), while a refill's net cash addition is `equity_after - equity_before`. A 97.90 → 1000 refill adds 902.10 to the cash ledger, even though the second stake makes the budget denominator 2000. Summing the last 100 closed rows would lose earlier trades and partial exits.
- **有效路径**：Use the complete refill records with the account balance: realized trading P&L = equity − initial equity − net refill cash. Keep backend `stakes`, `injected` and `multiple` as a separate capital-budget view. Analytics already excludes refills from `realized_pnl` and retains the pre-refill low in its curve; display its separate refill amount/count and explain that equity includes those additions.
- **通用规则**：When an account supports deposits, resets or refills, identify the cash-flow ledger before calculating performance. Never label balance recovery as trading profit. Test a nonzero residual before refill so stake budgeting cannot accidentally masquerade as actual cash flow.
- **牵连**：allin3 track 5060; snapshot `refills`, `stakes`, `injected`, `multiple`; analytics `refilled` and `refill_count`. Browser fixtures also verify 3× margin loss caps on full and partial close previews; backend execution and real account state are unchanged.
