# Small-risk and repeated entries cannot be rejected from intuition alone

- **Problem**: With the entry clock fixed to 5m, the owner wanted to identify entries to reject while preserving 10R/20R outcomes. Obvious candidates included small stops, old H1 watches, repeated arrows and candles that did not look like strong reversals.
- **Dead end**: A high fee/R ratio is a real cost burden but does not by itself identify the worst price-return cohort. Large R also mechanically favors small initial risks. Deleting those entries can sharply reduce cumulative losses in R while worsening average net price return and deleting the sought-after winners. Treating late entries as exhausted signals similarly confuses elapsed time or ordinal count with deterioration.
- **Effective path**: Freeze the 5m trigger and native execution ledger. Test ten individual causal vetoes, apply each to controls at their own entry times, and retain exact false-rejection lists. Compare net basis points, tail retention and trade retention; distinguish final gross R from maximum excursion. Choose on 2024 alone, then examine the same choice on 2025/2026 without retuning. Bootstrap whole weeks for the filter-minus-baseline contrast.
- **Evidence**: `exp-eth-5m-rejections-20261020-v1` reuses 4101 ETH signals. Baseline mean net return is -17.395bp. Requiring initial risk >=0.2% (cost <=1R under fixed 20bp costs) keeps 2037 trades, worsens mean to -18.357bp and retains only 18/53 final gross20R winners. Initial risk >=0.4% keeps none of those 53 winners. Entry ordinal >=7 contains 31/53 final gross20R winners and averages -16.622bp, versus -20.075bp for the first entry. These are independent overlapping entries, not independent market moves.
- **Failed improvement**: Rejecting dense repeats chooses the only 2024 arm meeting 80% tail retention and higher meanbp; its later mean is -16.290bp versus baseline -16.284bp. Rejecting countertrend entries still outside Monday's range improves full mean to -14.277bp but removes 13/53 final gross20R winners. The later gain is +3.779bp with a pointwise weekly 95% interval [0.562,7.232], yet Holm-adjusted p across ten candidates is 0.170. It is a research lead, not a validated hard veto. All ten filtered full-period means remain negative.
- **General rule**: Economic plausibility, visual confirmation and fewer trades are hypotheses. A production rejection needs evidence of incremental expectancy and tolerable false rejection of the target tail. Keep the entry clock fixed when investigating admission; do not silently replace it with a slower trigger or move stop/exit assumptions.
- **Scope**: New trailing 5m features only; 8202 native rows and all35 fields preserved, 55 summary rows, 20557 rematched pairs, 40 scalar probes, three prefix checks and16 behavioral tests. Nineteen unavailable Monday contexts pass that veto; no H4 warmup exclusion. No replay, data download, cost change, Pine update or live action.
