# Session-only launchd registration does not survive a machine restart

- **Problem**: After the Mac restarted on 2026-10-21 at 00:41:20 Asia/Shanghai, the signal center reported its OKX worker stopped. The website and monitor were running, but `com.fable.okx-execution.session` was absent from the launchd job list and the execution lock was free.
- **Dead end**: An independent launchd parent protects a worker from chat teardown, but does not imply login persistence. `RunAtLoad=true` only starts a loaded job; the existing plist was stored in the private runtime, absent from `~/Library/LaunchAgents`, and had `KeepAlive=false`. Website availability and account GET responses cannot establish execution liveness.
- **Effective path**: Identify the exact job and installation scope, then compare the read-only ledger with exchange positions and pending orders before preparing recovery. The existing preflight passed; 14 active lots matched 14 exchange positions, and 14 live stop orders matched the corresponding symbols, position sides, quantities, trigger prices, market execution and trigger type. Configuration and the original signal cutoff matched. Recovery was prepared but not executed at this checkpoint, pending the owner's explicit live-execution authorization.
- **General rule**: Distinguish three properties: independence from the launching chat, process restart policy, and login registration. Restoring a live execution worker may submit trades; permission to diagnose a stopped worker does not by itself choose a new persistent activation policy. Preserve the ledger, account identity, cutoff and strategy configuration; validate the owned PID, lock, advancing heartbeat and execution errors after an authorized restart.
- **Related**: `yoyo/okx_execution/README.md`, `__main__.py`, `dashboard.py`; private `OKXExecution/worker-session.plist`; [chat lifecycle independence](live-workers-must-outlive-chat-sessions.md). This diagnosis made no exchange writes and did not install login activation.

Owner subsequently authorized restoring this session with the original parameters. A fresh read-only preflight and the engine's full stop-ownership validator passed for all 14 lots before `launchctl bootstrap` loaded the original private plist. PID 5467 had PPID 1 and held the runtime lock. During initial catch-up, OL and POPCAT checkpoints were stale and the existing freshness gate paused entries; normal monitor scanning refreshed them without changing the gate or writing replacement market data. By 00:52:58 Asia/Shanghai, the worker had advancing heartbeats, no management or worker error, and an observed `accepting_entries=true`. The original cutoff, configuration and session-only policy remained. The private pre-recovery database and receipts are under `OKXExecution/recovery-20261021-reboot-1792515039/`.

The owner then requested recovery of the separate manual-account service. Its original `worker-manual.plist` had the same session-only registration. Read-only preflight confirmed a distinct account, two owned lots with valid OCO protection, no unconsumed entry confirmations and no pending management commands. The original job was bootstrapped as PID 6810, PPID 1, with the lock held and fresh error-free heartbeats. Both the readiness API and the owner's existing browser showed that order previews were available; COMP and TRUTH protection was verified. External SOL/ZEC inventory remained owner-managed. Manual runtime database backups and receipts are under `OKXManualExecution/recovery-20261021-reboot-1792515302/`; individual order confirmation and login activation policy were unchanged. Final checks found the original trade count and economic/state fields unchanged.
