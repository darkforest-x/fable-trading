# Live-executable Monday1618 entries compound to about 4x, not 109x: the touch-bar score leak lived in the compounding replays

- **Problem**: The spike paper score is known only at the close of the 5m bar that touched 1.618, too late to rest a live limit. The owner proposed layers: (A) rest on a pre-score from the previous close, (B) at the touch close, exit if the real score fails, (C) for levels touched with no resting order, enter at market at that close with the stop unchanged. Then combine the layers to keep return and cut drawdown. `exp-monday-limit-layered-20261027-v1`: NautilusTrader, both intrabar paths, v3 exits, base legs only. Each run used frac N20 3x sizing, 10 seeds, 10bp slippage on non-target exits and on late entries.
- **Dead ends**:
  - Reading per-trade means as the size of the problem. The causal plan A averages +146bp against +238bp for the leaky v3_be0 selection, which looks like a 40% haircut. In the compounding replay from 2020, however, the leaky selection reaches 109x with 30% drawdown and A reaches 3.9x with 77%. 2025 alone goes from 5.1x to 0.61x. The 243 events lost are big spike winners, and the 427 that enter are losers under v3 exits (−190bp). Compounding magnifies both.
  - Layer B as a free tail cut. Exiting the 427 pre-only fills at the touch close realizes the spike while it is through the level: −372bp against −190bp for holding them. It does trim the tail (≤ −1000bp: 63 → 49) and frees slots. In the replay, drawdown falls from 77 to 63% and the multiple rises slightly from 3.9 to 4.2x, but 2020–24 falls from 5.6 to 4.2x. That is a trade-off, not "return unchanged".
  - Layer C as recovering the leak. Market entry at the touch close on 227 of the 243 lost events (16 had already reached the target) earns +64bp, 50% win. The same events filled at the limit would have earned +321bp. The median entry is 1.8% beyond the level. C keeps a fifth of their edge.
  - A 1m spike entry layer. At the touch minute's close, 74 of the 243 have a partial score ≥ 0.5, and 53 of those are before the 5m close. The upper bound is about +136% of independent notional sum, against A's +3357%, so it is not worth a 1m-through-trade framework run.
- **What worked**: Run each layer as its own variant on identical events in the framework. Check that A reproduces the old ledger where nothing changed: 0 mismatches. Judge each layer on a compounding replay, not on per-trade means. ABC is the best causal combination over 2020–2026 (4.5x, 60% DD; 2025 0.97x). In the last five months A alone is best (1.86x, 16% DD vs 1.66x, 22%). Everything is in sample.
- **General rule**: When a selection leak is found, rerun the compounding replays on causal fills before quoting any multiple. A leak that moves per-trade means by a third can move compounded multiples by a factor of 25, because it removes exactly the large winners and admits losers. Exit filters that act at a bar close realize that bar's adverse excursion, so test them against holding on the same fills.
