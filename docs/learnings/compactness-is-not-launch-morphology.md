# Tightening MA width does not by itself recover the owner's launch morphology

- **问题**：SPIKE six-MA density admits about half of eligible bars. Does reducing its mean-width threshold identify the owner's true compact launch shape?
- **死胡同**：Treat retention reduction as improved accuracy, or infer that narrower is always better. At the archived unconfirmed proposal endpoints, width <=1 ATR removes all three owner-liked FIL/NEIRO/NMR references while retaining owner-rejected MMT/MASK proposals. Adding frequent body penetration also contradicts some reference windows that already show departure. These nine retrospective semantic examples are not confirmed core-coordinate gold and cannot support precision/recall.
- **有效路径**：Audit the actual SMA/EMA20/60/120 formula, split width, normalizer, topology and memory one at a time, and preserve the original sample identities and proposal boundaries. Use past-only charts to separate compact geometry, convergence trajectory, body interaction and subsequent release. Pair-cross count is not equivalent to group participation; fixed-identity nulls expose that distinction without inventing market labels.
- **通用规则**：Before selecting a morphology threshold, test whether it orders known semantic references consistently. If narrower rejected samples outrank accepted references, the missing axis is semantics or temporal alignment, not necessarily another width number. Do not tune to a tiny retrospective set, silently relabel it, or promote geometric retention to trading evidence.
