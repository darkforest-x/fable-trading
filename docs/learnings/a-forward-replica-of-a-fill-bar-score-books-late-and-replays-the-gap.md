# A forward replica of a fill-bar score books late and replays the gap

- **Problem**: The owner requires the Monday 1.618 paper accounts and every displayed score to match the original backtest, lookahead included. The backtest scores vol_ratio/liquidity/toward24 on the touch 5m bar, which is the fill bar. Since 10-25 the paper engine had scored on the bar before the touch, so its scores and P&L silently differed from the backtest it claimed to track.
- **Dead end**: Saying the pre-touch paper engine "is the executable version" answered a question the owner had not asked and contradicted their standing rule. Scoring at the touch instant cannot reproduce the backtest either, because the touch bar's close and full volume do not exist yet. On the backtest's own Binance bars (101 coins, 11,649 events) the pre-touch score passes 0.5 for 336 events against 271 stored, with a maximum difference of 0.54.
- **Effective path**: Store the live touch as `pending_score` with its levels. After OKX marks the touch 5m bar confirmed, compute the three features through that bar. Then open every account as the backtest books it, filled at the 1.618 in the touch minute, and replay the completed 1m candles from the touch minute up to scoring time before live quotes are read. Read the 1m candles before opening the accounts. Otherwise a failed read lets a live quote advance `last_seen_ms` past minutes that were never replayed. Treat candle read failures and an unconfirmed bar as retryable until a deadline; feature errors stay terminal. Parity of the new score on the same 11,649 events: median |diff| 0.0006, maximum 0.0065; the 0.5 cut passes 272 against 271 stored.
- **General rule**: When a forward account must follow a backtest whose decision reads the fill bar, delay the decision to that bar's close and book the fill backdated to the touch minute. Replay the gap in order, and label the account as a backtest replica, not executable orders. Check parity on the backtest's own bars before claiming that the live and backtest scores agree.
- **Dependencies**: `yoyo/monitor/monday_reclaim_paper.py` (`features(at_touch_close=True)`, `score_due`, `_open_accounts`), backtest definitions in `monday_limit_score_leak_audit.bar_features`, [notification freshness](notification-freshness-starts-when-the-score-can-exist.md). Rows scored before 2026-10-31 keep their pre-touch scores (`score_basis` NULL).
