# A recovery limit and a protective stop need different fee-neutral prices

- **Problem**: Gold 1.618 research asks for breakeven after a confirmation close, including fees, both when price reclaimed the level and when it remains underwater.
- **Dead end**: Placing the same stop at entry in both states is invalid. An underwater long already trades below a sell-stop breakeven trigger, so the order can execute immediately at a loss. Entry price also excludes both commissions. A close just inside the entry level can still be below the fee-neutral stop.
- **Effective path**: Use native STOP_MARKET after the close has passed the maker-entry/taker-exit breakeven price. An exactly equal close needs a native market exit: Nautilus rejects a stop amendment at its current quote (the first historical execution failed on this boundary; its log is retained). After a failed reclaim, keep the initial backstop and replace the profit target with a resting LIMIT at maker-entry/maker-exit breakeven. Use actual entry commission, native Money rounding and outward tick rounding. In the small reclaimed-but-inside-fee band, retain the backstop and use a strictly resting recovery limit at the taker-neutral price, recording that exception separately.
- **General rule**: Decide whether protection is a stop below the current long price or a recovery limit above it before computing costs. The order type and current executable side are part of the rule; a zero-return label is not evidence of fee coverage.
- **Related**: `yoyo/layers/l3_backtest/nautilus_gold_recovery.py`; `tests/evaluation/test_gold_recovery.py`; owner-fixed 1% backstop and actual OKX XAU fee group 4, read 2026-10-26. Native event excursions stop at the fill, so earlier entry-bar highs and later post-exit lows cannot inflate MFE/MAE.
