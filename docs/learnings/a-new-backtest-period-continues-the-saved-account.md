# A new backtest period continues the saved account; it is not a separate 1000U run

- **Problem**: The owner asked to run the live-wick strategies on October 2026. I replayed October as its own account from 1000U on 10-01 and put that month in an extra booklet table. The owner then opened the booklet and found no October in the equity curve, the monthly heatmap, the yearly bars or the trade replay. A "month result" that lives beside the backtest is not part of the backtest.
- **Dead ends**:
  - Treating the new month as a separate study (its own 1000U, its own table). The numbers were right, but they could not be compared with the curve the owner reads, and the compounding from the earlier equity was lost (6x3: +13 percent on 1000U vs +2,778U on the running 13,660U).
  - Concatenating the new rows by symbol into the saved rows. frac draws one random tie key per row position, so inserting rows in the middle would shift the keys of every later saved row and could reorder same-time entries in history.
- **What worked**: Append the new-period fills after all saved rows (a path-builder `extension` option), rebuild the same path outputs (ledger, history, manifest) as new versions, and require the saved prefix to match the old version exactly before using it. Here that was trade ids and equity, max diff < 1e-6. Then point the booklet and replay sources at the new versions, and let the chart reader append the extension's bars after the frozen series for those tracks.
- **General rule**: When the owner asks for "last month" of a running strategy, the deliverable is the existing account curve extended by that month (heatmap, yearly, trade list, charts), with the saved history proven unchanged. A standalone from-zero replay of the month is at most a side note.
