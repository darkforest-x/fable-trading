# Structure buffers must declare their price units across symbols

- **问题**：V2.3 carried an ETH-oriented absolute buffer of 1 price point onto OKX PEPE around 0.0000042 USDT. Long stops became negative and were rejected; short stops were around 1 USDT and rarely exited. Their oversized risk/reward fills overlapped across the visible chart.
- **死胡同**：Changing transparency or clipping boxes would only conceal the invalid scale. Reinterpreting every existing “1” as one tick would silently change the previously authorized ETH stop rule. Neither repairs the unit contract.
- **有效路径**：Make price points, minimum ticks, and entry-price percent explicit. Resolve to a price difference once at entry, then preserve outward tick rounding and frozen initial risk. Preserve the price-point option without silently reinterpreting it. The owner initially selected 1 tick, then explicitly corrected the request to percentage scaling with implementation discretion; the final default is 0.05% of entry close. Verify tiny-price long/short stops and R levels, zero buffer, percent rounding, and ETH price-point equivalence with native Pine fixtures.
- **通用规则**：Before carrying a numeric trading parameter across instruments, check its dimensions against both price and `syminfo.mintick`. Test both sides: a positive-stop guard can hide long failures while allowing absurd short risk. A scale warning must not silently substitute a different strategy parameter.
- **牵连**：`yoyo/evaluation/pine/monday_range_v2_4.pine`; `tests/evaluation/monday_range_v2_4_native_checks.pine`. Owner finally selected percentage scaling on 2026-10-20; 0.05% is a scale-normalizing starting parameter, not a profitability optimum. TradingView inputs follow the chart indicator when the symbol changes; they are not per-symbol presets. Official tick semantics: https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/concepts/chart-information/ .
