# Any-drawdown frequency does not justify passive scale-ins

- **问题**：V13.1 has 99.52% of trades experience some gross drawdown. Owner proposes half of the original quantity at entry and half on a resting order at minus 0.5 original R.
- **死胡同**：Treating any negative excursion as evidence that a meaningful retracement will improve entry ignores both depth and which trades receive the added exposure. Counting price touches as guaranteed fills also ignores queueing, partial fills and exit-order chronology.
- **有效路径**：Read the existing 21547-row excursion ledger. Only 2418/7311 eventual winners reached minus 0.5R, versus 13281/14236 eventual losers. Thus 4893 winners would receive no second leg under that price condition. These are retrospective path groups, not causal entry filters or a staged-order backtest. At exact half-R fills and the same exit, the proposed original-quantity split improves filled trades by 0.25 original R gross, but halves unfilled trades' results. Compare against both full size and constant half size to separate timing from exposure reduction.
- **通用规则**：Before proposing passive scale-ins from MAE frequencies, examine the eventual outcomes and opportunity cost of the filled and unfilled path groups. Freeze the original R denominator, absolute stop and exit clock in the first test. Recalculating the 2R trigger from a new average entry changes a second variable. Never use final MAE as an admission feature.
