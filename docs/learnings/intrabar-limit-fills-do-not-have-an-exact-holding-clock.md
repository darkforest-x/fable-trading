# An OHLC limit fill does not identify its elapsed-time exit price

- **问题**: The owner requested profitable-trade counts for a resting1.618 limit followed by a5-minute market exit. Most altcoin history contains5m OHLC, not the second of the first limit fill.
- **死胡同**: Closing at the end of the fill candle produces between0and5minutes of holding, not5. Assigning a made-up timestamp to an O-H-L-C path or adding one bar after a fabricated bar-close fill does not recover the requested duration. Minute data narrows this uncertainty but does not eliminate it for intraminute fills.
- **有效路径**: Keep the entry interval and distinguish opening fills from intrabar fills. For an entry in[T,T+d), the requested exit afterh belongs to[T+h,T+h+d). The observed open atT+h is a grid reference, while that future interval's low/high bound possible returns; reverse bounds for shorts and charge actual entry/exit fees on each price through the approved framework. Opening fills have exact grid timing and collapsed bounds. Refine with existing1m data only after its aggregated OHLC agrees with the registered5m source. Report a shared observed cohort across the authorized horizons and preserve missing expiry intervals.
- **通用规则**: Do not disguise data resolution as execution precision. Timing identification bounds are neither confidence intervals nor executable best/worst exit policies. Keep them distinct from statistical sampling uncertainty, and never select an exit from a future high/low as if it were a realizable strategy.
