# No initial stop requires an absent order and entry-notional returns

- **Problem**: A resting 1.618 entry waits through the immediately following timeframe candle before either exiting or arming an entry-price stop. Its initial risk distance is undefined.
- **Dead end**: The older no-stop-style helper used a remote finite protective price. Reusing that pattern would silently invent a maximum loss and an R denominator; arming a stop from a completed confirmation candle's earlier wick would also introduce retroactive execution. That helper was inspected, not used for this study.
- **Effective path**: Keep the native protective order absent until the actual fill-derived decision close. Native fills still own all exits. At the close, arm an exact-entry stop only after a strict reclaim; otherwise submit a market exit. Retain targets reached before confirmation. Normalize PnL by entry notional and label it explicitly; a price-flat exit remains net negative after two commissions.
- **General rule**: Specify order existence and effective event time before calculating performance. Do not manufacture risk units for strategies with no initial stop. Missing observations before a required decision close censor at the preceding observed boundary rather than postponing confirmation.
- **Scope**: `yoyo/layers/l3_backtest/nautilus_independent.py`, `yoyo/evaluation/monday_limit_reclaim.py`, `tests/evaluation/test_nautilus_limit_reclaim.py`; owner rules confirmed 2026-10-24. Nine native synthetic cases cover timing, gaps, fees and streaming continuity. Original historical studies are preserved.
