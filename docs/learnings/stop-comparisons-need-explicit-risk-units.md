# Stop comparisons must declare the risk unit and sizing interpretation

- **Problem**: The owner requested tighter stops. Describing the headline in the old risk unit answered a fixed-notional question instead of the owner's new-stop R question.
- **Dead end**: Keeping the old denominator avoids mechanical R expansion, but also hides the intended equal-price-risk comparison. Changing only the profit denominator while leaving costs in old R would be inconsistent.
- **Effective path**: Freeze causal entries and deadlines, execute each new stop through the existing native engine, and define primary R as absolute entry minus that variant's rounded initial stop. Divide both price PnL and unchanged notional costs by that risk. Retain old-risk and price-bp results only as diagnostics. In the two-year V2.37 stop-only/120-minute experiment, all 75/50/25-percent cuts worsened net R in both chronological years; the half-stop BTC 5m median fee rose from 0.885R to 1.769R, with 139 baseline net winners newly stopped.
- **General rule**: Declare the denominator and position-sizing interpretation before comparing stops. Equal price-risk normalization implies greater exposure as stops narrow, and does not cap fee-inclusive loss at 1R. Independent normalized trade sums are not funded-account returns. If TP or BE thresholds are present, state explicitly whether they move with the new R; this experiment has neither.
- **Scope**: `yoyo/evaluation/range_v237_smaller_stop.py`, `experiments/active/exp-range-v237-smaller-stop-20261022-v1/`; owner's new-R correction, frozen 20bp round-trip cost, 120-minute cap. This finding does not establish the outcome of different entry timing, costs, TP, or BE policies.
