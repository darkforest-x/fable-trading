# A shared payoff label can hide different return denominators

- **Problem**: The two Monday studies exposed similarly named payoff and profit-factor fields, but limit/reclaim used entry-notional returns while MA50 used original-risk R. A combined interim table could therefore imply comparability that did not exist.
- **Dead end**: Reusing field labels without their formulas, or choosing only the positive denomination, hides position-sizing effects. In the fixed 2026-10-24 22:24 snapshot, MA50 BTC/ETH averaged +11.16/+13.14 net bp per trade but -0.137/-0.456R. Winners had roughly twice the initial risk distance of losers; these are different weights, not contradictory fill ledgers.
- **Effective path**: Preserve the preregistered R measures and separately compute both studies' notional payoff as mean positive return divided by absolute mean negative return. Stream each completed asset once, aggregate additive counts/sums, exclude censored outcomes, separate gold gross returns, and reuse each study's complete 1+3 matching rules. Verify the new summaries against existing functions and fixed completed-file metadata without replaying candles.
- **General rule**: Name the denominator beside payoff, PF and mean return. A positive fixed-notional result must not silently replace a negative equal-risk result. Neither independent statistic is a funded account return; partial completion also is not random asset sampling.
