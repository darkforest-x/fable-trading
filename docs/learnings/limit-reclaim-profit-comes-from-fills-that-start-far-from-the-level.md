# Monday 1.618 limit-reclaim profit comes from fills that start far from the level, so live orders cannot wait for price to approach

- **Problem**: The owner asked how to run v3add20 live with 1000 USDT. About 500 OKX swaps × 2 weekly 1.618 levels is about 1000 levels. Resting a 50 USDT margin order on each would need about 50,000 USDT, so the obvious plan is to place orders only when price comes near a level.
- **Dead ends**:
  - Arming orders when price is within 1–2% of the level. On the 2108 v3 fills (score ≥ 0.5, `exp-monday-limit-v3-levels-20261025-v1`, worse intrabar path), the 5m close before the touch bar was a median 3.1% from the level. 51% of fills were more than 3% away and 35% more than 5% away, so price spiked to the level within a single 5m bar.
  - Those far fills carry the edge. Net bp per fill by distance at the previous close: <0.5%: −72 (111 fills); 0.5–1%: +59; 1–2%: +27; 2–3%: +96; 3–5%: +127; >5%: +565 (733 fills). A proximity rule keeps the weak fills and loses the profitable spike fills, unless it reacts within the seconds of the spike. 5m bars cannot measure that.
  - Trusting the paper engine for this. It fills on the first streamed touch, so it assumes the order was already resting and cannot see the problem.
- **What worked**: Score each level in advance instead of watching distance. The entry features (`features()` in `yoyo/monitor/monday_reclaim_paper.py`) use only candles closed before the touch's 5m bar. At every 5m close, the score that a touch in the next bar would receive is therefore already known. Only 3.6% of 58,732 historical touches score ≥ 0.5, so the levels worth a resting order are few. Rest orders on those levels in advance, up to the free slots.
- **Correction (same day)**: cluster24 and opp_first cannot change within the bar, because a fill's time is its touch bar's close. The backtest's stored scores are not causal, however: three of their features read the touch bar itself ([ledger-entry-i-is-the-fill-bar-not-the-submission-bar.md](ledger-entry-i-is-the-fill-bar-not-the-submission-bar.md)). The 2108 fills above were therefore selected with that leak. On the bar before the touch, 2293 events score ≥ 0.5.
- **General rule**: Before designing live order placement for a limit strategy, measure how far price was from the level one bar before each backtest fill, and the net result by that distance. If the edge sits in fills that arrive in one bar, the order must already rest, and the live question becomes how many orders must rest at once, not how close price is.
