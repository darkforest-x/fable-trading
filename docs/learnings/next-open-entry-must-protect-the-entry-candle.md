# Next-open entry must include protection during the entry candle

- **Problem**: The V2.37 confirmation experiment needs a market entry at the next observed 5m open, while the reusable Nautilus adapter normally enters at completed closes. Simply changing an entry index would change the fill price or omit the entry candle's stop risk.
- **Dead end**: Treating the next candle's close as its open would use the wrong price and clock. Using its high/low to admit or reject a trade would also make the entry depend on future price action. A close-only replay starting after that candle would miss genuine same-candle stops.
- **Effective path**: Keep the shared native adapter unchanged. Present each observed opening price as a point event, then the unchanged full OHLC candle at its close. A 1ns offset only orders the opening event after the preceding close. Entry happens on that point and a native protective stop exists before the remaining candle is processed. Restore logical open/close timestamps in the research output, and independently verify both directions, same-entry-candle stops, subsequent opening gaps, fees, and the exact 120-minute deadline.
- **General rule**: When moving an entry clock, verify the first tradable segment, not just the entry price. A bar-compressed replay must distinguish information used for admission from price evolution after the fill. The event ordering convention is not observed tick timing, latency, or evidence of available liquidity.
- **Scope**: range_v237_langlang_confirmation.py, its sixteen tests, and exp-range-v237-langlang-confirm-20261022-v1. This opening-point representation is used only for a fixed initial stop and a scheduled close exit; ATR, bar-count management, TP and other policies require separate validation.
